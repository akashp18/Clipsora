# Product Vision — Clipsora 2.0

Clipsora is a creative studio, not a button that makes clips. The user brings a long conversation; the studio
brings an editor's judgement, an art director's eye and a publisher's discipline, and hands back reels that
could have been made by a person who cares.

## The promise

1. Paste one podcast or upload one file.
2. Clipsora understands the whole conversation before it cuts anything.
3. It returns complete stories, each with its own visual identity, its own cover, and a publishing package
   written for the platform it is going to.
4. The user reviews, overrides anything they disagree with, and publishes.
5. Nothing is published, deleted or replaced without confirmation.

## The five rules the product is built on

1. **The story decides the length.** A 17-second statement and a two-minute story are both correct answers.
   No target duration, no padding, no cutting a payoff to fit a template.
2. **Meaning before style.** Typography, colour and motion are chosen from what the clip *is*, not from a
   random preset rotation. Five systems, one per kind of conversation, each versioned and reviewable.
3. **One brand, many looks.** Reels from the same podcast may differ in treatment; they must still be
   recognisably the same channel. The Brand Kit is the source of that consistency.
4. **Platforms are different audiences.** YouTube and Instagram get separate titles, descriptions, tags, CTAs
   and cover crops. Same clip, different package.
5. **Automatic by default, never opaque.** Every automatic decision is visible and reversible: the direction,
   the boundaries, the cover, the score, the publishing copy.

## Who it is for

- A podcast editor who publishes six clips a week and is tired of scrubbing timelines.
- A creator-run channel with a house style that must survive across editors.
- A small studio running several shows and needing one place to keep them apart.

## What "premium" means here

Restraint. Fewer, better decisions: one strong cover instead of four mediocre ones; one CTA instead of three;
one creative direction per clip instead of every effect switched on. Deep charcoal, warm white, one accent.
Typography and spacing carry the design, not shadows and gradients.

## Success looks like

- A user uploads a 100-minute episode, does something else, and comes back to reels that do not need re-cutting.
- The first three seconds of every reel are the speaker, not a title card.
- Every cover reads at thumbnail size.
- No reel ends mid-sentence.
- The same brand looks like the same brand six weeks apart.
- Failures are visible: a clip that could not be made says why, and can be retried.

## What it is not

- Not an NLE. It is a caption/hook/cover compositor with an editor, not a keyframe animation suite.
- Not a virality oracle. Scores are editorial estimates, and are labelled as such.
- Not a bot farm. Publishing is confirmed by the platform before it is counted as published.
