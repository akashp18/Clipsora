# Branding

## 1. Naming — ten candidates

| # | Name | Reads as | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shortsmith** | shorts + smith (a maker) | says the category and the craft; works as a verb ("smith it"); strong GitHub org | smith/forge names are common; needs a search |
| 2 | **Offcut** | the piece cut from a longer film | short, memorable, design-literate, ownable | slightly obscure to non-editors |
| 3 | **Clipwright** | clip + wright (maker) | precise, self-hosted craft feel | "wright" reads archaic to some |
| 4 | **Slatecut** | slate (film) + cut | cinematic, short | two hard consonants, weaker recall |
| 5 | **Reelcast** | reel + broadcast | hints at live + publishing | suggests a streaming service, not a tool |
| 6 | **Framecut** | frame + cut | technical, clear | generic-sounding |
| 7 | **Streamcut** | stream + cut | nails the live use case | narrows the product to streamers |
| 8 | **Cutline** | the line where you cut | editorial, quiet, premium | weak association with video |
| 9 | **Clipforge** | clip + forge | energetic, maker-ish | heavily used pattern; likely collisions |
| 10 | **Vantage** | a good position/view | premium, short | generic; unrelated to clipping |

**The name is Clipsora.** A coined word (clip + -sora): short, spellable from hearing, and it works as a GitHub org. The checks below still apply before public use.

**Not verified:** domain availability, GitHub org availability, trademark conflicts, and app-store/social handle availability. Before any public use, run: registrar + WHOIS for `.com`/`.app`/`.dev`, GitHub org slug, EUIPO/USPTO quick search, and a plain web search for existing products. Do not print "available" anywhere until that is done.

## 2. Licence and attribution

`LICENSE` holds the MIT notice that ships with every copy of this project. That is the single place the record
lives: nothing in the product, the pages or these docs repeats it, and no marketing copy implies that anyone else
endorses this version.

## 3. Identity

**Personality:** precise, calm, craft-proud. An editor's tool, not a growth-hack toy. Confident but never hypes outcomes.

**Tagline options**
1. "Your footage. Your AI. Your clips." — leads with ownership.
2. "Unlimited source. Explainable cuts." — leads with the two differentiators.
3. "A clipping studio that shows its work." — the explainability promise.

Recommendation for Clipsora: **"A clipping studio that shows its work."** with "Your footage. Your AI. Your clips." as the sub-headline. The wordmark is set in Archivo Black, lowercase `clipsora`, with the dot of the `i` replaced by the cut-mark glyph.

**Colour:** keep the warm cream + burnt orange from the current build as the light theme; dark theme is a graphite/ember pair. Orange reads as "record"/"cut" and is already consistent across seven pages, so it is retained rather than replaced — continuity is a brand asset.

**Typography:** Archivo Black for display (already loaded), Geist for UI, Anton/Poppins for caption presets. No new font licence.

**Logo direction:** a single glyph — an open bracket/frame with a diagonal cut through it, drawn as a 24 px stroke-free mark, same family as the existing inline icon set. Must work at 16 px (favicon) and one colour. No gradients, no mascots.

**Built:** the mark is a terracotta tile with its top-right corner sliced off, a cream play triangle inside — the frame and the cut in one shape, two colours, no gradient, still readable at 16 px. The wordmark is `clipsora` in Manrope 800, converted to outlines so it never depends on a font being installed. The files are `web/brand/favicon.svg` (the mark, also used beside the name in the sidebar), `web/brand/logo.svg` (lockup for light pages), `web/brand/logo-light.svg` (the same lockup for dark pages) and `web/brand/logo-email.png` (the lockup as a picture, because email apps do not show SVG). Pages ask for them as `/brand/…?v=N`; raise N when the artwork changes, since brand files are cached for a week.

**Voice:** short sentences, verbs first, no adjective stacking. Never promise views, virality or income. Say "the AI proposes; you decide."
